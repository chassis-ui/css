---
'@chassis-ui/css': minor
---

**Breaking:** Chassis CSS is built with `@chassis-ui/tokens` 0.10, which names the font families by their job and gives every text role its own size and line height. The HTML font tokens, the size scale of the display family and five unused roles are gone.

- The family for components is `ui`, the name of `text` until now: `$font-family-ui`, the `"ui"` key of `$font-families`, `--cx-font-family-ui` and `.font-ui` (`font-ui` in the Tailwind entry). Its scale is renamed with it: `$font-size-ui-large` and the other sizes, weights and line heights, the maps `$ui-font-sizes`, `$ui-font-weights` and `$ui-line-heights`, the custom properties `--cx-font-size-ui-lg`, `--cx-font-weight-ui-strong`, `--cx-line-height-ui-lg` and the others, and the font maps `$font-ui-large-normal` and the others. `$font-size-lg`, `--cx-font-size-lg`, `.font-lg` and the other names without a family keep their names and point at the `ui` scale.
- `.font-text` and `--cx-font-family-text` (`var(--cx-font-family-ui)`) stay in this minor as deprecated names of `.font-ui` and `--cx-font-family-ui`, and are removed in the next breaking minor. The Sass variable `$font-family-text` is removed.
- New family for reading content: `prose`, with `$font-family-prose`, `--cx-font-family-prose` and `.font-prose`, a scale of ten sizes and line heights and four weights (`$prose-font-sizes`, `$prose-font-weights`, `$prose-line-heights`, `--cx-font-size-prose-lg` and the others), and the compound utilities `.font-prose.font-lg` and `.font-prose.font-strong` beside the ones of `code`. In the default tokens `prose` is the typeface of `ui`, with the same sizes.
- The display family has no scale of its own. Removed: `$font-size-display-*`, `$line-height-display-*`, `$display-font-sizes`, `$display-line-heights`, `--cx-font-size-display-*`, `--cx-line-height-display-*` and the compound utilities `.font-display.font-lg` and the other sizes, with no replacement: use a text role, or `.font-prose` with a size. `.font-display`, `.font-display.font-strong` and the other weights stay.
- The HTML font tokens are removed: `$font-size-html-*`, `$line-height-html-*`, `$font-weight-html-*`, `$font-family-html-heading`, `$html-font-sizes`, `$html-font-weights`, `$html-line-heights`, the font maps `$font-html-*` and the 32 `--cx-*-html-*` custom properties. The headings, `.blockquote` and `.attribution` take their sizes from the text roles, with the values they had.
- `$context-fonts` holds 20 text roles: `jumbo`, `hero`, `h1` to `h6`, `lead`, `blockquote`, `cite`, and three sizes each of `title`, `body` and `label`. Their font maps are `$font-h1`, `$font-lead`, `$font-title-large` and the others (`$font-context-*` until now, and `$font-blockquote` and `$font-cite` for `$font-html-blockquote` and `$font-html-cite`). The `heading`, `highlight`, `link`, `expired` and `code` roles are removed, and `.font-heading` with them: use `.font-h1` to `.font-h6`.
- `:root` declares the size and line height of each role, `--cx-font-size-h1`, `--cx-line-height-title-lg` and the others (jumbo and hero keep their sizes in their classes), and the family and weight of each role group: `--cx-font-family-heading`, `--cx-font-weight-title` and the others of `jumbo`, `hero`, `heading`, `lead`, `body`, `title`, `label`, `blockquote` and `cite`. The Sass variables are `$font-size-h1`, `$line-height-h1`, `$font-family-heading`, `$font-weight-heading` and the others, in the maps `$context-font-sizes`, `$context-line-heights`, `$context-font-families`, `$context-font-weights` and `$context-font-groups`.
- New classes `.font-h1` to `.font-h6`, `.font-blockquote` and `.font-cite`. The headings style `h1`, `.font-h1` and `.h1` together; `.h1` to `.h6` are deprecated and removed in the next breaking minor.
- A role class reads the family and weight of its role group, so `--cx-font-family-title` changes every title class and `--cx-font-family-heading` every heading. It sets `font-style`, `text-transform`, `text-decoration` and `letter-spacing` only where the role gives them a value: the italic of `.font-blockquote`, the letter spacing of `.font-jumbo` and `.font-hero`. A role class inside `<em>` or an underlined link now keeps the italic or the underline it inherits.
- `$heading-font-family` is `var(--font-family-heading)`, `$heading-font-weight` is `var(--font-weight-heading)`, `$h1-font-size` is `var(--font-size-h1)` and `$h1-line-height` is `var(--line-height-h1)`, and the same for the other levels. The custom properties `--cx-h1-font-size`, `--cx-h1-line-height` and the other levels, `--cx-heading-font-family` and `--cx-heading-font-weight` are no longer read: set `--cx-font-size-h1` or `--cx-font-family-heading` instead. `$bold-font-weight` is `var(--font-weight-mass)`, `700` as before.
- With tokens 0.10, the title classes of the `chassis` brand use the display family, Montserrat, instead of Inter, with the weight they had.
- A project with its own token source builds it with `@chassis-ui/tokens` 0.10. `scss/tokens/` reads `$cx-typography-font-family-ui`, the `prose` scale and the `context` roles, which 0.9 does not have.
