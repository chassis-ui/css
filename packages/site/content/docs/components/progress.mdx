## Introduction

Progress bars show how much of a task or process is complete. The component is built from two `<div>` elements instead of the HTML `<progress>` element, which makes room for labels inside the bar, stacked segments, and striped or animated backgrounds.

### Key features

The component is made of these parts:

- **Wrapper** (`.progress`) — the track, which carries the `progressbar` role and the ARIA values.
- **Bar** (`.progress-bar`) — the filled part, whose width shows the progress.
- **Stack** (`.progress-stacked`) — a track that holds several segments.
- **Modifiers** (`.striped`, `.animated`) — a striped background on the bar, and its animation.

## Basic structure

Wrap a `.progress-bar` in a `.progress` element. The wrapper carries `role="progressbar"`, a label, and the `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`, and `aria-valuemax` attributes; the width of the bar shows the value and is set separately, here with an inline style.

<Example code={`<div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Basic example" aria-valuenow="0" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar" style="width: 0%"></div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Basic example" aria-valuenow="25" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar" style="width: 25%"></div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Basic example" aria-valuenow="50" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar" style="width: 50%"></div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Basic example" aria-valuenow="75" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar" style="width: 75%"></div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Basic example" aria-valuenow="100" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar" style="width: 100%"></div>
  </div>`} />

## Width

Set the width of the bar with an inline style, a [sizing utility]([[docsref:/utilities/sizing]]), or custom CSS. Keep it in step with `aria-valuenow`.

<Example code={`<div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Basic example" aria-valuenow="75" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar w-75"></div>
  </div>`} />

## Height

Set a `height` on the `.progress` wrapper to change the height of the track. The `.progress-bar` inside stretches to fill it.

<Example code={`<div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Example 1px high" aria-valuenow="25" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" style="height: 1px">
    <div class="progress-bar" style="width: 25%"></div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Example 20px high" aria-valuenow="25" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" style="height: 20px">
    <div class="progress-bar" style="width: 25%"></div>
  </div>`} />

## Labels

Place text inside the `.progress-bar` element to label it. The label is centered in the bar by default and takes the foreground color of the bar.

<Example code={`<div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Example with label" aria-valuenow="25" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar text-end" style="width: 25%"><span class="mx-2xs">25%</span></div>
  </div>`} />

### Long labels

The `.progress-bar` element has `overflow: hidden`, which cuts off a label that is wider than the bar. An [overflow utility]([[docsref:/utilities/overflow]]) such as `.overflow-visible` lets the label extend past the bar.

<Callout type="warning">
  A label that extends past the bar sits partly on the `.progress` background and partly on the `.progress-bar` color, and may not have enough contrast with both. When the length of a label varies, place it outside the progress bar.
</Callout>

## Colors

The bar uses the primary color by default. Apply a [background utility]([[docsref:/utilities/colors]]) to the `.progress-bar` to give it the color of another context.

<Example code={`<div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Success example" aria-valuenow="25" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar bg-success" style="width: 25%"></div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Info example" aria-valuenow="50" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar bg-info" style="width: 50%"></div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Warning example" aria-valuenow="75" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar bg-warning" style="width: 75%"></div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Danger example" aria-valuenow="100" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar bg-danger" style="width: 100%"></div>
  </div>`} />

<Callout name="warning-color-assistive-technologies" />

### Label contrast

A label on a bar with a background utility needs a matching foreground color. Add the `.fg-contrast` class of the [contrast helper]([[docsref:/helpers/contrast]]) so the label stays readable on the background.

<Example code={`<div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Success example" aria-valuenow="25" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar fg-contrast bg-success" style="width: 25%">25%</div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Info example" aria-valuenow="50" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar fg-contrast bg-info" style="width: 50%">50%</div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Warning example" aria-valuenow="75" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar fg-contrast bg-warning" style="width: 75%">75%</div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Danger example" aria-valuenow="100" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar fg-contrast bg-danger" style="width: 100%">100%</div>
  </div>`} />

## Stacked bars

Place several `.progress` elements in a `.progress-stacked` container to show segments in one track. In a stack the width goes on each `.progress` element, whose bar fills it, and each segment keeps its own role, label, and ARIA values.

<Example code={`<div class="progress-stacked">
    <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Segment one" aria-valuenow="15" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" style="width: 15%">
      <div class="progress-bar"></div>
    </div>
    <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Segment two" aria-valuenow="30" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" style="width: 30%">
      <div class="progress-bar bg-success"></div>
    </div>
    <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Segment three" aria-valuenow="20" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" style="width: 20%">
      <div class="progress-bar bg-info"></div>
    </div>
  </div>`} />

## Striped bars

Add `.striped` to a `.progress-bar` to lay a striped gradient over its background color.

<Example code={`<div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Default striped example" aria-valuenow="10" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar striped" style="width: 10%"></div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Success striped example" aria-valuenow="25" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar striped bg-success" style="width: 25%"></div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Info striped example" aria-valuenow="50" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar striped bg-info" style="width: 50%"></div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Warning striped example" aria-valuenow="75" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar striped bg-warning" style="width: 75%"></div>
  </div>
  <div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Danger striped example" aria-valuenow="100" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar striped bg-danger" style="width: 100%"></div>
  </div>`} />

### Animated stripes

Add `.animated` to a striped bar to move the stripes along the bar with a CSS animation.

<Example code={`<div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Animated striped example" aria-valuenow="75" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
    <div class="progress-bar striped animated" style="width: 75%"></div>
  </div>`} />

<Callout name="info-prefersreducedmotion" />

## Accessibility

A progress bar built from `<div>` elements has no native semantics, so the markup has to supply them:

- Add `role="progressbar"` to the `.progress` wrapper, not to the bar.
- Label the wrapper with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
- Set `aria-valuenow` to the current value, and `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax` to the range. Update `aria-valuenow` together with the width of the bar.
- Check the contrast of a label against the bar, and of the bar against the track, when a background utility or a custom color is used.
